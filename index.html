The story

Three weeks was all it took.

One morning, the sun changed

The ozone layer tore open in three weeks. Daylight became a killer, and the cities emptied.

The survivors went underground

Metro tunnels and mine shafts became shelters. You lead one of them.

Those left in the light didn't die

They changed. Now they roam the surface at night — and they hear everything.

It was them

Ships in orbit turned the sun into a weapon. At dawn they drop their robots to claim the surface.

The last shuttle

The robots sealed a colony shuttle at the Spaceport. Repair it, launch it, and lead your people to a new world.

What you'll do

Greed by night, home by dawn.

Night raids

Go up after dark, loot crates and buildings, and race the clock back to the elevator before sunrise burns you.

Legendary loot

Rare crates, legendary weapons, gear and blueprints — every night a gamble between greed and fear.

Mutants and robots

Fight with guns, grenades, EMP and smoke — or sneak past in the shadows. A squad of soldiers can join you.

Underground colony

Dig a three-floor shelter for up to 200 people: generators, water, greenhouses, barns, labs, barracks.

Families and choices

Children grow up, elders age, crises come. Every choice has a price.

Defend the shelter

When the horde comes, your guards hold the entrance and reinforcements run from the barracks.

FAQ

Which platforms?

Android first (Google Play). iOS and PC are planned.

Is it free? Are there ads?

It's free, with no ads and nothing that sells power.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline; your saves stay on your device.

English, Türkçe, Deutsch, 日本語, 한국어 and Français.
